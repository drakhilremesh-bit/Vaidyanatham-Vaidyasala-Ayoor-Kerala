Vaidynatham Herbals is rooted in the sacred Angadi Marunnu tradition since 1872, carrying forward the healing legacy of V. Sukumaran Vaidyan and the revered Kalaripurayidathu lineage.

Built on the principle that true healing begins at the root, our approach recognises the importance of digestion, nourishment, vitality, strength and resilience in holistic wellbeing.

We believe in the wisdom of nature. Each formulation is part of a living tradition — developed with respect for heritage while presented with clarity and responsibility for today's families.
